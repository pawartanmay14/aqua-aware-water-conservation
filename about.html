About AquaAware

Water conservation and responsible water stewardship.

Our Vision

A world where every person has access to reliable, safe and sustainable water, where communities thrive through responsible water stewardship and conservation efforts.

A future where innovation and awareness protect our water sources for generations to come.

Our Mission

To protect, manage and conserve water resources by promoting responsible water use, sustainable access to clean water and community engagement.

Core Values

  • Sustainability: preserving water resources for future generations.
  • Awareness: encouraging informed water habits.
  • Conservation: reducing unnecessary water waste.
  • Community: working together to protect water resources.
